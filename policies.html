Check-in & check-out Cancellation Rates & taxes Rooms Force majeure

01

Check-in & check-out

  • Check-inFrom 12:00 PM
  • Check-outBy 10:00 AM
  • Late check-outUntil 4:00 PM · 50% charge

02

Cancellation

The charge depends on how long before your arrival date you cancel.

30 days or more before arrival No charge
15 to 29 days before arrival 50% of the stay
14 days or less before arrival Full stay charged

03

Rates & taxes

  • All room rates are net and include government taxes.
  • Rates do not include the Government Environment Levy of €2 per person, per night. It is paid directly at the hotel.
  • The hotel may adjust its rates if government taxes change.

04

Rooms

  • We have one accessible Double Room, available on request and subject to availability.
  • Rooms are on the ground floor and the first floor.
  • We can't guarantee a room's location, but you can ask for the ground or first floor when you book (in "Special requests"). We'll do our best, subject to availability.

05

Force majeure

The hotel may cancel a booking, with written notice and without liability, because of events beyond its reasonable control, such as natural disasters, acts of government authorities or other emergencies.
